AI-Powered Lead Scoring for ViciDial Campaigns

Turn evidence from conversations into explainable lead priorities, with clear handling for missing information and human overrides.

Lead scoring can help a ViciDial team decide which eligible opportunities deserve attention first. A useful score explains which facts contributed to the ranking and how recent those facts are. An unsupported number is difficult for an agent to trust or correct.

Define the decision the score will support

Start with a concrete question, such as which requested callbacks should appear first in a supervisor's review queue. Decide who can act on that priority and when the score expires. Eligibility and suppression still control who can be contacted.

Separate facts into fields the team can verify. Useful inputs might include a stated implementation timeframe, a confirmed service requirement, and a request to speak with a specialist. Record whether the caller stated the information directly or the integration inferred it.

Start with a small, explainable rubric

For an illustrative telecom campaign, assign two points for an explicitly requested quote, one for a confirmed target date, and one for a stated need that matches an available service. Send records with missing or contradictory information to review when those facts matter to the decision.

Use the AI to extract candidate facts and retain the source call reference. Apply the scoring rubric in the integration so the same inputs produce the same priority. Preserve the rubric version and allow a supervisor to correct a score with a reason.

Avoid attributes unrelated to the service decision. A customer's speech pattern or the model's guess about their income is a poor basis for prioritizing a telecom inquiry.

Write the right data to the right record

Store the score, confirmed facts, evidence reference, calculation time, and version. Match results to the lead and specific attempt before applying an update. If two calls produce updates in reverse order, protect the newer evidence and any human correction.

ViciDial's Non-Agent API documents lead and custom-field operations. Confirm available fields and permissions in the installation, then decide whether the score belongs in ViciDial, the CRM, or an integration store. Verify how the team will use it before letting it affect any dialing order. ViciDial Non-Agent API

Check whether the score improves decisions

Run the first version in observation mode. Compare its recommendations with human review and completed outcomes. Examine a sample of low-priority records as well as high-priority ones so missed opportunities stay visible.

Watch how performance changes by lead source and how it changes over time. A rule that works well for inbound quote requests may work differently on an older callback list. Recheck the rules when the campaign, product, or collection process changes.

For how teams can act on the result, see automating lead qualification with a sales team. Contact HiDial to discuss where lead evidence and routing decisions fit into your workflow.
