Automated Call Quality Assurance: What AI Can Review and What Supervisors Should Verify

Use AI to assist call reviews with evidence-based criteria, supervisor calibration, correction paths, and separate handling of technical uncertainty.

Automated quality assurance can help supervisors locate conversations that need attention and prepare draft evaluations. It should not turn every uncertain transcription or model judgment into a confirmed agent-performance issue.

A useful QA workflow separates observable evidence, interpretation, and the final decision. The technology can assist the review, while supervisors remain responsible for the standards and how results affect people and customer operations.

Define criteria that reviewers can apply

Begin with the existing quality form. Rewrite vague questions such as “Was the agent professional?” into clearer criteria that explain which behavior is being evaluated and what evidence is required.

For example, check whether the business was identified, whether the customer's request was acknowledged, or whether a stated next step matched the action documented in the system. Some questions need the recording, others need case data, and others cannot be answered from the transcript alone.

Include an insufficient-evidence outcome. A missing sentence in a transcript should not automatically count as proof that the agent failed to say it.

Use AI for draft answers and review queues

A model may propose answers to a defined evaluation form or flag a conversation for inspection. Require the result to point to the relevant conversation evidence rather than provide only a score.

Amazon Connect documents generative AI performance evaluations, including review before submission and limitations linked to transcript quality. Treat it as an example of an available platform capability, not evidence that every call-center system offers equivalent accuracy or coverage.

For ViciDial, verify how recordings, call identifiers, lead records, and review results would move between the dialer and any external QA service.

Separate technical problems from agent behavior

Poor audio, overlapping speakers, an unsupported language, or an incomplete recording can distort the evaluation. Route those cases for technical or manual review instead of presenting a numerical result as dependable.

Do not infer intent or dishonesty from a sentiment label. A frustrated customer, a difficult connection, and an agent's actual conduct require different kinds of evidence.

Similarly, a keyword match may help find a call but does not necessarily explain the surrounding context. Supervisors should listen to the relevant passage before confirming a serious finding.

Calibrate with human reviewers

Choose a representative sample and have trained reviewers evaluate it using the same definitions. Compare disagreements at the question level rather than only comparing total scores.

Identify whether the difference came from unclear criteria, missing context, transcript errors, or the AI's interpretation. Update the form or workflow accordingly and keep the version used for each evaluation.

Recheck the process after a model, template, or campaign change. A configuration that worked for one call type may not apply to another.

Give agents a correction path

Explain which evidence supports a finding and allow the agent to raise a concern through an established review process. Keep the original automated suggestion distinguishable from the final approved evaluation.

Prioritize review before using uncertain outputs for consequential decisions. Automated summaries and scores should help supervisors investigate, not obscure responsibility for the final judgment.

Track overturned findings and the reasons for correction. They can reveal systematic problems that a dashboard average would hide.

Evaluate coverage and usefulness honestly

Report how many calls were eligible for automated review, how many produced usable results, and how many required manual attention. A large recording inventory does not mean the entire inventory was evaluated successfully.

Measure time spent verifying results, consistency between reviewers, and whether coaching addresses observable behavior. Continue sampling unflagged calls so the team can detect issues the automated process missed.

Start with limited call types and maintain manual evaluation while the pilot is assessed. Read about AI call summaries for ViciDial, or contact HiDial to discuss the recording and integration requirements for your environment.
