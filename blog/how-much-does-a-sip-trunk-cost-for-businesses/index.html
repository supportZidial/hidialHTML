How Much Does a SIP Trunk Cost for Businesses?

SIP trunk pricing depends on your calling workload and the provider's billing model.

SIP trunk pricing depends on your calling workload and the provider's billing model. A small office making occasional calls has different requirements from a contact center running outbound campaigns throughout the day.

Compare complete quotes rather than assuming a per-minute rate is the full cost. Confirm that the quoted service accepts your traffic type before calculating savings.

Understand the billing model

A per-channel plan charges for concurrent call paths. A metered plan charges for usage, often alongside a monthly account or number fee. Some providers combine capacity commitments and usage charges.

An advertised unlimited plan may have destination limits, fair-use conditions, or restrictions on automated traffic. Ask which calls are included and what happens when the account reaches its capacity or usage limit.

Estimate your actual traffic

Start with monthly inbound and outbound minutes, destination countries, toll-free usage, and peak simultaneous calls. For dialers, include the rate of new attempts as well as connected calls. Calls per second and concurrent channels measure different demands.

Use representative call detail records where possible. Separate normal periods from seasonal peaks so a temporary campaign does not dictate a year-round commitment unnecessarily.

Check billing increments

Ask how calls are rounded and whether a minimum charge applies. A rate billed in full-minute increments can produce a different invoice from the same rate billed in smaller increments, particularly when calls are short.

Clarify which unsuccessful attempts are charged, how premium or special destinations are handled, and whether connection fees apply. Use the provider's actual rate sheet for your destination mix.

Include number and service charges

A realistic budget can include:

  • Number setup and monthly rental.
  • Inbound usage, outbound usage, and forwarding.
  • Porting or provisioning charges where applicable.
  • Capacity commitments and optional support arrangements.
  • Taxes, surcharges, and currency conversion where applicable.

Request these items in writing. There is no single percentage that describes every business's telecom taxes or savings.

Compare against your current service

Use the same traffic sample to estimate both services. Include any contract termination charges, overlapping service during migration, and changes to network or phone-system costs.

A lower rate can help, but failed calls, poor audio, or an unsuitable support process can create operational costs elsewhere. Check the connection with a controlled pilot before moving all traffic.
